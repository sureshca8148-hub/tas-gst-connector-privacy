TAS GST Connector — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

1. Purpose

TAS GST Connector connects the TAS Pro website with supported GST portal pages through the user's browser. It is designed to assist users with GST-related workflows using their own browser session.

2. Information and permissions

The extension does not request credit card details or intentionally collect general browsing history. When users use its features, it may access GST portal page content or related information necessary to provide the requested connection.

3. Use of information

Information accessed by the extension is used to provide the connector functionality requested by the user. The extension is not intended to track unrelated browsing activity or use information for unrelated advertising.

4. Transmission and storage

The extension may communicate with the TAS Pro website and supported GST portal pages to provide its features. Any transmission, storage, retention or deletion of information must follow the actual operation of the extension and TAS Pro service.

5. Sharing and sale

TAS GST Connector does not sell users' personal information. Information should not be shared for purposes unrelated to providing the requested functionality.

6. Security

Users should protect their GST portal credentials and keep their devices secure. Reasonable safeguards should be applied to information handled by the extension and its associated service.

7. User control

Users can disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome's extension settings. Uninstalling the extension does not necessarily remove information previously stored by the TAS Pro service.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The current version will be available at this page.
